About SmartBite

SmartBite is a budget-aware nutrition planner built for everyday Indian eating. It combines your body metrics (BMI, BMR, TDEE) with a database of realistically priced meals and their ingredient quantities to build a 7-day menu sized to your calories, protein target, and monthly food budget — then checks the real grocery cost of repeating that week for a month against your budget before showing you anything.

How your data is stored

This version runs as a real web application with its own server and database (SQLite, in the file smartbite.db next to the app). When you sign up, your password is hashed with Werkzeug's PBKDF2-SHA256 (salted, one-way — never stored as plain text) and saved in the users table. Logging in checks your password against that hash and starts a signed session cookie; your profile, meal plans, grocery lists, weight log, and reports are all tied to your user ID and reloaded automatically each time you log back in.

Not medical advice

This tool, including the AI assistant, gives general nutrition and budgeting guidance only. It isn't a substitute for advice from a doctor or registered dietitian — especially if you have a diagnosed medical condition.
